### Aura of Protection
*2nd-level Spell (Abjuration, Aura)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Components:** V, M (a "Shield")  

🔷 **(duration: up to 1 hour, requires: {*Concentration*, you are wielding a "Shield"})** — Creatures of your choice within a 30 ft. sphere centered on and originating from you gain a `+X` bonus to their *AC*, where `X = your Spellcasting Ability modifier`.

**Classes:** Paladin
