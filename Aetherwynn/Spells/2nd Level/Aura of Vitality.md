### Aura of Vitality
*2nd-level Spell (Abjuration, Aura, Therapeuomancy)*  
**Cost:** `3` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S  

🔷🔷🔷 **(duration: up to 1 minute, requires: *Concentration*)** — At the end of each of your turns until this "Spell" ends, any number of target creatures in a 30 ft. sphere centered on and originating from you regain `Xd6` *HP*, where `X = this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Cleric, Paladin
