### Quagmire
*2nd-level Spell (Transmutation)*  
**Cost:** `3` "Mana"  
**Components:** V, S  

🔷🔷 **(range: 120 ft., requires: Concentration, duration: 1 minute)** — Target 20 ft. × 20 ft. square of ground becomes *Difficult Terrain*. Creatures inside this area sink 2 ft down into the ground. Each creature that starts its turn in this area makes an *Athletics (Strength) Check*.

**Failure:** The creature is *Restrained I* until the start of its next turn.  

**Classes:** Druid, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
