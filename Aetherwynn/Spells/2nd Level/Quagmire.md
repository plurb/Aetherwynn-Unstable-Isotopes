### Quagmire
*2nd-level Spell (Transmutation, Geomancy)*  
**Cost:** `3` "Mana"  
**Components:** V, S  

> *"There was a mage who was famous for his use of this spell. Can't seem to remember his name..." — Varek Thorne*

🔷🔷 **(range: 120 ft., duration: 1 minute)** — Target 20 ft. × 20 ft. square of ground becomes *Difficult Terrain*. Creatures inside this area sink 2 ft. down into the ground. Each creature that starts its turn in this area makes an *Athletics (Strength) Check*.

**Failure:** The creature is *Restrained I* until the start of its next turn.  

**Classes:** Druid, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
