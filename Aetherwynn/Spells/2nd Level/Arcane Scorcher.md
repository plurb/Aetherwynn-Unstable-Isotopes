### Arcane Scorcher  
*2nd-level Spell (Evocation, Blast)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (a red dragon's scale)  

> *You fire a jet of roaring flame from your hands.*

🔷🔷 — Make a *Ranged Spell Attack* vs. *Reflex* in a 60 ft. long and 5 ft. wide beam originating from you.

**Success:** You deal `Xd8 [fire]` *Damage*.  
**Failure:** You deal half as much *Damage*.  

Where `X = 2 + this Spell's level`

**Classes:** Aethersmith, Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard
