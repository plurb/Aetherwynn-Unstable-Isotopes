### Silence
*2nd-level Spell (Abjuration)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S  

> *You point at a creature and utter a divine command, compelling them to remain silent.*

🔷🔷 **(range: 60 ft., requires: *Concentration*, duration: 1 minute)** — Make a total of up to `X` *Ranged Spell Attacks* vs. *Will* against up to `X` different target creatures in range. You have *Disadvantage* on this *Attack* if the creature cannot hear you. You gain *Advantage* on this *Attack* if the creature has "Keen Hearing".

**Success:** The creature is *Silenced* until this "Spell" ends.  
**Failure:** You deal `2d6 [psychic]` *Damage*, and it is not *Silenced*.  

Where `X = this Spell's level - 1`.

**Classes:** Cleric, Paladin
