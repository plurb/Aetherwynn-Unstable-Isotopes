### Searing Strike  
*2nd-level Spell (Evocation, Barrage)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S  

> *You unleash a barrage of radiant bolts, searing flesh and destroying undead.*

🔷🔷 **(range: 150 ft.)** — Make a total of up to `X` *Ranged Spell Attacks* vs. *AC* against up to `X` target creatures in range, where `X = 1 + this Spell's level`.

**Success:** You deal `1d10 [radiant]` *Damage*. If the creature is "Undead", then it takes twice as much *Damage*, and its *Maximum HP* is reduced by the same amount.

**Classes:** Cleric, Magus, Paladin, Wizard
