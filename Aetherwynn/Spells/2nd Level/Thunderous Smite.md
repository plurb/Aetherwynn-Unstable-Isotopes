### Thunderous Smite
*2nd-level Spell (Evocation)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V  

◻️ **(1/turn)** — When you hit a creature with a *Weapon Attack*, the creature takes an additional `Xd6 [thunder]` *Damage*, and the creature is knocked up to `10 * X` ft. away from you and falls *Prone*, where `X = 1 + this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Magus, Paladin
