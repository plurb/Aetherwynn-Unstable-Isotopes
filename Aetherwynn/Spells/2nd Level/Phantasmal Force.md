### Phantasmal Force  
*2nd-level Spell (Illusion, Curse)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `2` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fleece)  

> *You create an image of fear and dread in the mind of your target.*

🔷🔷 **(requries: *Concentration*, range: 60 ft., duration: up to 1 minute)** — Make a *Ranged Spell Attack* vs. *Will* against up to `X` different target creatures you can see in range, where `X = this spell's level - 1`.

**Success:** The creature is *Blinded*, and *Frightened* of itself until this "Spell" ends.  

A creature affected by this "Spell" can use 🔷 to make an *Investigation (Intelligence) Check*.

**Success:** The effects of this "Spell" end for that creature.

**Classes:** Psion, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
