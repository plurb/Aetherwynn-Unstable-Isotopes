### Disperse
*2nd-level Spell (Abjuration)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S  

⚡ **(range: 60 ft.)** — If you see a creature within range "Cast a Spell", *[Counter]* target "Conjuration" or "Necromancy" "Spell" of level `X` or lower, where `X = 1 + this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Druid, Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard, Warlock
