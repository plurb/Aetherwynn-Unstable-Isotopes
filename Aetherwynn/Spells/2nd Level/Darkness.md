### Darkness  
*2nd-level Spell (Evocation)*  
**Cost:** `1` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (bat fur and a drop of pitch or piece of coal)  

> *"Lights off."*

🔷 **(range: 60 ft., duration: up to 1 minute, requires: *Concentration*)** — You create a 15 ft. radius sphere centered on and originating from target point or object that is *Small* or smaller in range. The area inside this sphere is filled with *Magical Darkness*.

If any of this "Spell's" area overlaps with an area of light created by a "Spell" of level `X` or lower, the "Spell" that created the light is *Dispelled*, where `X = this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Magus, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
