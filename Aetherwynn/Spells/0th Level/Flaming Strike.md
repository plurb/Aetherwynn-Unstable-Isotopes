### Flaming Strike
*Cantrip (Evocation, Pyromancy)*  
**Cost:** —  
**Components:** —  

> *You infuse an attack with burning elemental energy.*

🔷 **(1/turn)** — Make a *Melee Weapon Attack* using a "Melee Weapon" you are wielding, or an "Unarmed Strike" if you are not wielding a weapon. This *Attack* deals an additional `Xd6 [fire]` *Damage*. If the *Attack* succeeds, then another target creature within your "Reach" takes `Yd6 [fire]` *Damage*.

Where `X = 0` starting at 1st level, `X = 1` starting at 5th level, `X = 2` starting at 11th level, and `X = 3` starting at 17th level. And where `Y = X + 1`.

**Classes:** Aethersmith, Magus, Paladin, Ranger
