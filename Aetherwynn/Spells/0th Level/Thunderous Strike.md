### Thunderous Strike
*Cantrip (Evocation, Bronteomancy)*  
**Cost:** —  
**Components:** S, M (a "Melee Weapon" worth at least 1 sp)  

> *You infuse an attack with booming energy.*

🔷 **(1/turn)** — Make a *Melee Weapon Attack* using a "Melee Weapon" you are wielding, or an "Unarmed Strike" if you are not wielding a weapon. This *Attack* deals an additional `Xd8 [thunder]` *Damage*. If the *Attack* succeeds, then until the end of your next turn, if the affected creature moves 5 ft. or more, you deal `Yd8 [thunder]` *Damage* to it.

Where `X = 0` starting at 1st level, `X = 1` starting at 5th level, `X = 2` starting at 11th level, and `X = 3` starting at 17th level. And where `Y = X + 1`.

**Classes.** Artificer, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
