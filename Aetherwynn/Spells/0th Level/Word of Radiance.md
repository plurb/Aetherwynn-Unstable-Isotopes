### Word of Radiance
*Cantrip (Evocation, Lumomancy)*
**Cost:** —  
**Components:** V, M (A Holy Symbol)

> *Blah blah blah...*

🔷🔷 **(range: 10 ft.)** — Make a *Melee Spell Attack* vs. *Reflex* against any number of creatures of your choice in range.

**Success:** You deal `Xd6 [radiant]` *Damage*.

Where `X = 2` starting at 1st level, `X = 3` starting at 5th level, `X = 4` starting at 11th level, and `X = 5` starting at 17th level.

**Classes.** Cleric, Paladin
