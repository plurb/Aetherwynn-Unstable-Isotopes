### Branding Smite
*1st-level Spell (Evocation)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V  

◻️ **(1/turn, duration: 1 minute)** — When you hit a creature with a *Weapon Attack*, the creature takes an additional `Xd6 [fire]` *Damage*. If the creature is *Invisible*, then the ability granting the creature *Invisible* ends. If the creature is *Hidden* from any creatures, then it is no longer *Hidden* from those creatures. Until this "Spell" ends, the creature has *Disadvantage* on *Stealth Checks* it makes, cannot become *Invisible*, and *Attacks* targetting the creature gain *Advantage*.

Where `X = 1 + this Spell's level`

**Classes:** Magus, Paladin, Ranger
