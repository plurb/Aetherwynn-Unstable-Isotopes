### Feather Fall
*1st-level Spell (Transmutation)*  
**Cost:** `1` "Mana"  
**Components:** V, M (a small feather or a piece of down)  

> *You slow your or your ally's descent.*

⚡ **(range: 60 ft., duration: 1 round)** If you or a creature within range falls 5 ft. or more, then you may reduce *Damage* from "Falling" of up to `X` target creatures in range to `0` until end of turn.

Where `X = your Spellcasting modifier`.

**Classes:** Artificer, Bard, Druid, Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard
