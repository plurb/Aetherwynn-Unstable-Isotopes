### Scorching Ray  
*1st-level Spell (Evocation, Pyromancy, Barrage)*  
**Cost:** `1` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S  

> *Gray dashes to cover. The barrage of missiles too great to defend head on. He peeks out from behind the boulder, just fast enough to unleash three motes of flame.*

🔷🔷 **(range: 120 ft.)** — Make a total of up to `X` *Ranged Spell Attacks* vs. *AC* against up to `X` target creatures or objects that aren't being worn or carried in range, where `X = 2 + this spell's level`.

**Success:** the target takes `2d6 [fire]` *Damage*.

**Classes:** Artificer, Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard
