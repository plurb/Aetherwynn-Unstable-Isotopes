### Piercing Ray
*1st-level Spell (Evocation, Barrage)*  
**Cost:** `1` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M  

> *Ordinary offensive magic.*

🔷 **(range: 150 ft., requires: you are wielding a "Staff" or "Wand")** — Make a total of up to `X` *Ranged Spell Attacks* vs. *AC* against up to `X` target creatures in range. These *Attacks* ignore "Magical" bonuses to the target's *AC*.

**Success:** You deal `2d6 [force]` *Damage*.

Where `X = this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Magus, Sorcerer, Wizard
