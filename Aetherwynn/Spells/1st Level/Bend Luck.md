### Bend Luck  
*1st-level Spell (Divination)*  
**Cost:** `3` "Mana", `1` "Four-Leaf Clover"  
**Components:** V  

> *You pluck the strings of fate, hoping for a more favourable outcome.*

⚡ **(range: 60 ft.)** If a creature in range makes an *Attack Roll*, or *Ability Check*, after the creature makes its *Attack Roll*, or *Ability Check* but before success is determined, roll a d20. The triggering creature uses your choice of the new roll or its previous roll.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
