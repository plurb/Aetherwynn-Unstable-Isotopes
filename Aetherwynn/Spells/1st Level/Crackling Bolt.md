### Crackling Bolt
*1st-level Spell (Evocation)*  
**Cost:** `2` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (a twig from a tree that has been struck by lightning)  

> *"Unlimited power!!!" - Some guy.*

◻️ **(1/turn, duration: up to 1 minute, requries: *Concentration*)** — Until this "Spell" ends, you gain the following ability:
> 🔷 **(1/turn, range: 120 ft.)** — Make a *Ranged Spell Attack* vs. *AC* against target creature in range. **Success:** You deal `Xd12 [lightning]` *Damage*, where `X = this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Artificer, Druid, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
