### Mage Armour  
*1st-level Spell (Abjuration)*  
**Cost:** `1` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (a piece of cured leather)  

> *You apply an invisible protective barrier of aether to yourself.*

🔷🔷🔷 **(range: touch, duration: 8 hours)** — Target creature in range has *AC* `10 + X + your Dexterity modifier` while not wearing "Armour" until this "Spell" ends.

Where `X = 2 + this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Artificer, Magus, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
