### Sleep  
*1st-level Spell (Enchantment)*  
**Cost:** `1` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of fine sand)  

> *"Perhaps you'd like a blanket?"*

🔷🔷🔷 **(range: 60 ft., duration: up to 1 minute, requires: *Concentration*)** — The greatest number of creatures in a 10 ft. sphere centered on and originating from target point in range whose total remaining *HP* equals up to `Xd8` fall *[Asleep]*, where `X = 2 + this Spell's level`.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
