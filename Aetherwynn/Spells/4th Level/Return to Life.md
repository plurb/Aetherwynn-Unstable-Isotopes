### Return to Life
*4th-level Spell (Conjuration)*  
**Cost:** `7` "Mana", A *Legendary Item*  
**Components:** V, M (A *Legendary Item*)  

> *You pray to your god to summon your soul back to the material world in case of your death.*

🔷🔷🔷 **(duration: 1 week)** — Until this "Spell" ends, if you would die, you *Ressurect* yourself seven days later instead. You gain the effects of the "Freedom of Movement" "Spell" for 1 hour if you do *Ressurect* this way.

**Classes:** Cleric
