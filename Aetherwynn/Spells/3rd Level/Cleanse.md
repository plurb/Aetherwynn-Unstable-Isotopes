### Cleanse
*3rd-level Spell (Evocation)*  
**Cost:** `3` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** V, S, M (holy water worth 10 gp)  

> *You summon holy light, cleansing the world of what would defile its order, leaving no trace of their existence.*

🔷🔷 **(range: 150 ft.)** — Make a *Ranged Spell Attack* vs. *Will* against "Aberrations", "Fey", "Fiends", "Horrors", "Monstrosities", and "Undead" in a 30 ft. sphere *Blast* centred on and originating from target point in range.

**Success:** You deal `Xd8 [radiant]` *Damage* ignoring *Resistance* and *Immunity*, where `X = 2 * this Spell's level`.
**Failure:** You deal half as much *Damage* ignoring *Resistance*, and *Immunity*.

A creature killed this way does not leave a corpse, and cannot be *Ressurected* except through the "True Ressurection" "Spell".

**Classes:** Cleric, Paladin
