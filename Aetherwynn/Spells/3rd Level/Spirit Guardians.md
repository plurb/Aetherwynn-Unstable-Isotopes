### Spirit Guardians
*3rd-level Spell (Conjuration)*  
**Cost:** `3` "Mana"  
**Overload:** `1` "Mana" per level  
**Components:** C, V, S, M (ashes [consumed])  

> *Spectral forms of protectors and warriors heed your call for aid.*

🔷🔷 **(requires: *Concentration*, duration: up to 1 min.)** — You gain the following effects until this "Spell" ends:
* The area within a 10 ft. sphere centred on you is *Difficult Terrain*.
* ⬜ **(1/turn)** — At the end of your turn, make a *Melee Spell Attack* vs. *Fortitude* against creatures in a 10 ft. sphere centred on and originating from you.

  **Success:** `Xd8 [radiant or cold (your choice)]` *Damage*, where `X = this Spell's level - 1`.  
  **Failure:** You deal half as much *Damage*.  

**Classes:** Cleric, Paladin
