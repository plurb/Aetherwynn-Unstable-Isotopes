### Aura of Resilience
*3rd-level Spell (Abjuration, Aura)*  
**Cost:** `4` "Mana"  
**Components:** V  

🔷 **(requries: *Concentration*, duration: up to 1 hour)** — Creatures of your choice within a 30 ft. sphere centred on and originating from you gain a `+X` bonus to their *Reflex*, *Fortitude*, and *Will*, where `X = your Spellcasting Ability modifier`.

**Classes:** Paladin
